# Gig Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026 (Workforce WS sign-in)_

Gig Tracker is a browser extension that checks the production-crew job boards
you choose and shows you which listings are new.

## What it reads

When you run a check, Gig Tracker opens the job-board pages you have listed
in its settings, in your own browser, using the logins you already have with
those sites. It reads the listings on those pages: titles, dates, locations,
roles and pay rates. For the Workforce WS board it asks that company's own
worker portal for the same information, using the sign-in your browser
already has with it.

## Where that information goes

Nowhere but your own browser. Your settings and the listings it has found
are kept in your browser's extension storage. If you are signed in to your
browser's sync feature, your settings travel with your browser account the
same way your bookmarks do.

Gig Tracker has no servers of its own. It does not send your listings,
settings, logins, browsing history or anything else to the developer or to
anyone else. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising, and nothing
is sold or shared.

## Passwords

Gig Tracker never sees or stores your passwords. You sign in to each job
board yourself, on that board's own page.

For Workforce WS, signing in gives your browser a temporary sign-in pass.
Gig Tracker keeps a copy of that pass in its own storage on your computer,
so later checks don't need you to sign in again until it expires. It is
only ever sent back to the Workforce WS portal itself.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Forget everything** in the extension erases everything it has
stored. Removing the extension erases it too.
